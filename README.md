## 📁 Featured Projects

### 🚗 NCR Ride Business Performance Analysis  

**Problem:**  
Understand operational efficiency and revenue patterns from large-scale ride data.

**Data:**  
150,000+ Uber ride booking records (raw, imperfect dataset)

**What I Did:**
- Cleaned, structured, and validated raw data using Excel & Power Query  
- Performed exploratory data analysis to identify trends and inconsistencies  
- Built Power BI dashboards to track performance and revenue insights  

**Key Insights:**
- Identified peak demand periods and underperforming time windows  
- Highlighted efficiency gaps affecting revenue growth  

**Limitations & Assumptions:**
- Dataset lacked customer demographic details  
- Assumed consistency in pricing logic across regions  

📌 *Outcome:* Delivered clear, decision-oriented dashboards suitable for business stakeholders.

### 🤖 AI Impact on Jobs by 2030
**Tools:** Excel, Power BI  

**Problem:**  
Help job seekers understand which roles face high AI automation risk and which show growth potential.

**What I Did:**
- Cleaned and transformed job-market datasets  
- Analyzed automation exposure across industries  
- Designed a Power BI dashboard showing risk levels and income brackets  

**Key Insights:**
- Identified job roles with low automation risk and strong growth outlook  
- Highlighted skills linked to higher long-term resilience  

**Limitations & Assumptions:**
- Projections based on secondary datasets and trend assumptions  
- Results reflect probabilities, not certainties  

📌 *Outcome:* Provided practical, forward-looking insights to support career decisions.
